Screen Guard Edge Tester

Version 7.0

Measure how many display pixels are hidden by your screen protector.

This tool draws a thin green line from the edge of your display, allowing you to accurately determine how much of the active screen is covered.

For Best Accuracy

  • Use your browser at 100% zoom.
  • Use Fullscreen mode before measuring.

Recommended Browser
Google Chrome
